Limits & honesty

Mathcracked BSc is a syllabus-focused trainer, not a claim that every possible problem is solved.

Supported BSc modules in this build

  • Calculus (chain/product/quotient, parts, partial fractions), linear algebra (det, inverse, Gaussian, eigenvalues), DE, series, Bayes, induction & classical proofs
  • Expand / simplify patterns for common binomials
  • Derivative power rule examples
  • Standard algebraic identity proofs with plan + formal lines
  • Curated example bank (calculation + proof)

Not claimed

  • Arbitrary word problems without structure
  • University real-analysis ε–δ proofs
  • Abstract algebra or topology
Design principle: if a step cannot be justified reliably, the app should say so rather than invent working.
